Popular Destinations

Discover the most beautiful places Himachal Pradesh has to offer.

  • Rohtang Pass

    One of India's most iconic mountain passes — snow-covered and breathtaking all year round.

  • Chandratal Lake

    The "Moon Lake" of Spiti — a pristine high-altitude lake surrounded by barren mountains.

  • Triund Trek

    A beginner-friendly trek near Dharamshala offering panoramic views of the Dhauladhar range.

  • Great Himalayan Park

    A UNESCO World Heritage Site — home to rare wildlife and untouched Himalayan forests.

  • Kullu Valley

    Known as the Valley of Gods — famous for the Dussehra festival and Beas River rafting.

  • Kinnaur

    Apple orchards, ancient temples, and the stunning Kinnaur Kailash peak — a hidden gem of HP.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your Himachal trip.

What is the best time to visit Himachal Pradesh?

March to June is ideal for sightseeing and pleasant weather. For snow activities, visit December to February. Spiti Valley is best explored from June to September when the roads are open.

Do you offer customised tour packages?

Yes! We specialise in fully customised itineraries. Tell us your travel dates, budget and interests, and our team will craft a personalised package just for you.

Are the packages suitable for families and senior citizens?

Absolutely. We have family-friendly packages with comfortable stays, easy-paced itineraries and vehicles with experienced hill drivers. Senior-citizen-friendly options are available on request.

How do I make a booking or payment?

Simply contact us via the form below, email or phone. We confirm your itinerary first, then you can pay via UPI, bank transfer or card. An advance of 25% confirms your booking.

Do you arrange permits for places like Rohtang Pass?

Yes, we handle all required permits (Rohtang, Inner Line for Spiti, etc.) as part of the package so you can travel worry-free.
