Popular Destinations

Discover the most breathtaking places in Himachal Pradesh, from snow passes to alpine lakes.

Where To Go

Handpicked destinations that showcase the true beauty of the Himalayas.

  • Rohtang Pass

    One of India's most iconic mountain passes — snow-covered and breathtaking all year round.

  • Chandratal Lake

    The "Moon Lake" of Spiti — a pristine high-altitude lake surrounded by barren mountains.

  • Triund Trek

    A beginner-friendly trek near Dharamshala offering panoramic views of the Dhauladhar range.

  • Great Himalayan Park

    A UNESCO World Heritage Site — home to rare wildlife and untouched Himalayan forests.

  • Kullu Valley

    Known as the Valley of Gods — famous for the Dussehra festival and Beas River rafting.

  • Kinnaur

    Apple orchards, ancient temples, and the stunning Kinnaur Kailash peak — a hidden gem of HP.
