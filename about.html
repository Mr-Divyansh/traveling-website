About Us

The story, mission and people behind Himachal Pradesh Tourism.

Our Story

Himachal Pradesh Tourism was born out of a shared love for the mountains and a dream to share the magic of Himachal with the world. What began as a small group of enthusiastic explorers has today grown into a trusted travel companion for hundreds of happy travelers.

We believe travel is not just about visiting new places, it is about experiencing new cultures, trying new foods, meeting new people, and collecting memories that last a lifetime. That is why every tour we plan is crafted with care, passion, and a personal touch.

Our Mission

To deliver unforgettable travel experiences in Himachal Pradesh that are safe, affordable, and deeply connected to the local culture and nature. We aim to make the beauty of the Himalayas accessible to everyone while supporting sustainable and responsible tourism.

Our Vision

To become Himachal Pradesh most loved and recommended travel brand, known for honest service, unique itineraries, and a genuine love for the mountains. We envision a future where every traveler explores Himachal responsibly and returns home with a piece of the mountains in their heart.

Meet the Founder

Rajesh Sharma — Founder & CEO of Himachal Pradesh Tourism. With years of experience in travel planning and a deep love for the mountains, Rajesh leads the team in creating memorable experiences for every traveler who visits the land of mountains.
